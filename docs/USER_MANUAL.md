# 📖 User & Caregiver Manual

Welcome to the **Atypical Speech Assistant**! This application is designed to be warm, friendly, and non-technical.

---

## 🎤 Tab 1: Speak & Check
1. Click on the **🎤 Speak & Check** tab.
2. Select your preferred input method (**Microphone** or **File Upload**).
3. Press the microphone button to record your speech, or upload an audio recording.
4. The system will transcribe your spoken words and display:
   - **Disorder Classification**: Typical or Dysarthria.
   - **Severity Category**: Mild (Amber), Moderate (Orange), or Severe (Red).
   - **Model Confidence & Severity Percentage**.
5. Click **📄 Download PDF Report** to save a clinical summary, or **💬 Share on WhatsApp** to send your transcript to family!

---

## 🏋️ Tab 2: Practice & Training
1. Choose an exercise difficulty (**Easy**, **Medium**, or **Hard**).
2. Read the **Target Phrase** displayed in the blue card.
3. Record your vocal attempt whenever you are ready. There are no timers or pressure!
4. Receive instant, encouraging feedback and a match percentage score.
5. In the **📹 Webcam Video Exercises** sub-tab, upload a webcam video to generate overlaid captions on your practice video!

---

## 📈 Tab 3: My Progress
1. View your **Severity Reduction Trend Chart** to see your improvement over sessions and days.
2. Check your daily practice streak and unlock achievement badges as you practice consistently.

---

## 💬 Tab 4: Ask AI
1. Type or speak questions about dysarthria, vocal practice, or app features.
2. Click any of the **Suggested Question Chips** for quick answers!

---

## 📤 Tab 5: Share & Contacts
1. Select a recent speech check or enter a message.
2. Click **General WhatsApp Share** to pick a contact in WhatsApp.
3. Use the **Saved Contacts List** to add caregiver names and phone numbers for one-click direct sharing!
